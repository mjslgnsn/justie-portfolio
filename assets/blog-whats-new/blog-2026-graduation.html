Starting Again,
But Stronger

Graduation. Sana. Malapit na. But for now, I'm just taking it one step at a time and trusting that I'm on the right path — because this time, I know what I'm made of.

The end of one chapter. The blank page of the next.

This chapter feels like 2023 again

There's something poetic about how the end of a journey can feel exactly like its beginning. Standing here, on the edge of graduating, I feel that same mix of uncertainty and hope I had walking into college as a freshman. New, a little uncertain — but different this time.

In 2023, I didn't know who I was yet. I was figuring it out. Now? I've been Vice President, College Governor, award recipient, competition winner, intern. I've led. I've failed. I've rebuilt. And I'm still here.

The difference between who I was in 2023 and who I am now isn't just experience — it's the quiet confidence that comes from choosing to keep going, even when it was hard.

What this milestone means to me

Graduation isn't just a ceremony — it's proof. Proof that I showed up even when I was exhausted. Proof that I didn't quit when the pressure was overwhelming. Proof that every sleepless night, every difficult decision, every moment I doubted myself — it was building toward something real.

I'm not saying I have everything figured out. I don't. But I've learned that you don't need to have everything figured out to keep moving forward. You just need to trust the version of yourself you've become.

Let's see where this goes

Malapit na ang chapter na iyon — the real world, the full version. And honestly? I'm excited. Nervous, yes. But excited more than afraid.

This is me, starting again. But stronger.
