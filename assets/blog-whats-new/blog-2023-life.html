Trying, Even When
I'm Not Ready

2023 felt like a fresh start — and honestly, nakakatakot. But I chose to try anyway. I said yes to opportunities, joined activities, and slowly learned that kaya ko pala.

First year. First steps out of the comfort zone.

Everything was new

Walking into college as a freshman in 2023, the world felt enormous. New campus, new people, new expectations, new version of myself I hadn't figured out yet. Everything was unfamiliar in the best and most unsettling way.

There were days I felt completely out of place — like everyone else had a script and I hadn't been handed mine. But I kept showing up. Because what's the alternative? Go home? No. Try, even when it's uncomfortable.

Hindi siya madali — but doon ko nakita na kaya ko pala. The moment I stopped waiting to feel "ready" and just moved, everything shifted.

Saying yes to things that scared me

I made a quiet decision early in 2023: say yes more than no. Join things. Try out. Show up for opportunities even if I wasn't sure I was qualified. And that decision changed the entire shape of this year.

I joined activities I didn't fully understand yet. I introduced myself to people who intimidated me. I raised my hand in rooms where I wasn't sure I belonged. And slowly, I started to realize — I did belong. I just needed to act like I did first.

What I discovered

The biggest thing 2023 taught me is that readiness is a myth. Nobody feels fully ready for the things that matter. You just decide to move, and readiness builds along the way.

By the end of the year, I wasn't the same person who walked in. I was louder in the right rooms, braver in the right moments, and more certain than ever that trying — even poorly, even uncertainly — is always worth it.
